The Big 3 For Consoles:

Despite the NES appearing to be the odd one out based on era, these three consoles alone were absolute unites in the gaming battlefield! Theres a reason why your older relatives refer to any gaming console as : "The Nintendo." You might have noticed the exclusion of the Wii in the "Big Three" tier list and the reasoning for that was simple, the system just simply didn't have the same level of horsepower compared to the Xbox 360 or PlayStation 3 when it came to hardware. Both were just constantly at each other's throats in competition, whereas Nintendo was more so viewed as the Baby's First Console.
