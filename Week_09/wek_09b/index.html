Remembering The Beasts Of Each Era

Playstation 3
The Sony PS3 was capable of FREE online play, online streaming services, digital copies of games, and PS1/PS2 backwards compatibility from earlier versions of the system.
Xbox 360
The Xbox 360 was also capable of using online streaming services, online play and backwards compatibility, however those two unique features were limited to the Xbox Live Subscription. However this system had the stronger graphics in comparison to the PS3.
Nintendo Entertainment System
While Atari may had brought gaming to life in the 70's, the NES launched the gaming industry through the roof! endless iconic entries, iconic gaming accessories such as the Zapper, and most importantly, the father to the biggest game series: Super Mario Brothers.

The Big 3 For Consoles:

Despite the NES appearing to be the odd one out based on era, these three consoles alone were absolute unites in the gaming battlefield! Theres a reason why your older relatives refer to any gaming console as : "The Nintendo." You might have noticed the exclusion of the Wii in the "Big Three" tier list and the reasoning for that was simple, the system just simply didn't have the same level of horsepower compared to the Xbox 360 or PlayStation 3 when it came to hardware. Both were just constantly at each other's throats in competition, whereas Nintendo was more so viewed as the Baby's First Console.

Game Boy Advance SP
The GBA SP was a handheld that was able to play the entire Game Boy library, and it was also the first handheld to have a rechargeable battery.
PlayStation Vita
The PS Vita was a handheld that was able to play games that were on par with the PlayStation 3, and it also had a built-in camera and touch screen/ back touch pad.
Playstation Portable
The PSP was a handheld that was able to play games that were on par with the PlayStation 2, and it also had a built-in web browser.

The absolute MONSTERS for Handheld Gaming:

The Game Boy Advance SP, PlayStation Portable, and PlayStation Vita were all absolute beasts in their own right. All three of these handhelds were able to provide gamers with an experience that was on par with their home console counterparts.
