Young Sheldon REVIEWED

Understanding The Spin-Off

Young Sheldon is the spin-off show to hit show "Big Bang Theory," sharing main character; Sheldon Cooper's, VERY interesting childhood.

The Show follows a classic Sitcom recipe in which Sheldon; the established gifted child in the Cooper household, faces an issue either involving his age like in episodes acknowledging how odd it is that a nine year-old attends high school, or it could be Sheldon facing his fears that seem miniscule to his peers, and more often than not the audience. This Show despite having a very simple sitcom recipe, keeps you interested by raising stakes between characters; whether it be a love triangle between the adults, or the Cooper family's values being challenged by their children like in many episodes where Sheldon and Mary have thrown feuds over their beliefs involving religion.
