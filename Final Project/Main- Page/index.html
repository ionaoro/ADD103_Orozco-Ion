What's a GREAT game for Beginners?

On this website, I will serve as your personal guide to getting started on your gaming journey! One of the most important aspects in gaming is finding your own identity within the games that you play, and also the many types of games that our out there! Above are games for the curious, and those who have never touched a controller before!

BEST beginner friendly game genres:

Platformers These either 3D or 2D styled games are excellent for beginners as the controls are often simple but gameplay is still challenging RPGs These games are intended for players that prefer a much longer runtime, whether it's maxing out your in-game stats, or exploring the lore to a game to it's fullest extent. Fighting Games Maybe you prefer to play with friends in more competitive settings? Fighting games are often easy to pick up and play, but hard to master.
