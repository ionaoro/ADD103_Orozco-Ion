About DOOMThird major release from id Software.

Doom is well known for popularizing, and creating a true fandom for the First-Person Shooter genre. Despite not being the first EVER FPS game, it definitely was the stick of dynamite that would go on to catapult the genre into becoming the colossal icon that it is now. DOOM was responsible for providing the gaming world with true online multiplayer, and on primitive hardware nonetheless!

About Heavyweight ChampThe first ever fighter game.

Heavyweight Champ released in 1976, was a pioneering arcade game that helped establish the Arcade Fighter genre. This game in particular was most unique for it's choice of controller, being that the controller was a special pair of boxing gloves that players would put on!
